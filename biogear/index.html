About the game

Where biology meets machinery.

In Maher BioGear the hero doesn't fly a plane — he rides his weapon. The BioGun is a huge flying cannon where every part can be upgraded. Steer with the touch stick, fire at the targets filling the sky and grab the items that help you along the way.

Choose Story Mode to follow Maher's journey, or Arcade Mode to test your skill.

Gameplay

Build your BioGun.

✦

➹

⚙

Craft new weapons and keep them in your weapons inventory.

Collectibles

The story

A boy who loved the mountains.

In a very far-away country that blends advanced technology with beautiful nature lived a boy called Maher, who loved mountain climbing. His adventure starts there — between futuristic cities and the mountains — alongside Dr. Karim and Dr. Samy.
