About the game

Roll out. Hold the line.

In Nawyah you drive a tank across arenas built from isometric tiles — brick, white stone, iron, water and trees. Your tank rolls out of its garage when the doors open, then you blast your way through walls and face waves of enemy tanks.

Before each fight you choose from several tank designs, each with its own balance of power, speed and recharge time — and you pick its colour yourself.

Features

What's inside.

◈

Choose your tank

Several tank designs with different power, speed and recharge stats — and a colour picker to make it yours.

⇄

Play together

Two players on one screen with keyboard or controllers (PS5 controller supported), or online with host-and-join multiplayer.

☉

Enemies with personality

Smart tanks hunt you down, fast ones dart around, cowards run away, heavy ones grind slowly forward and dumb ones just wander.

▦

Destructible arenas

Brick, white stone and iron blocks shrink with every hit until they break — iron takes the most punishment. Water and borders shape the map.

⌂

Garage launches

Every tank starts inside a garage — the door swings open and it drives out onto the battlefield.

✎

Hand-drawn art

Tanks and tiles are drawn by hand, from the first line sketches to the final colours.
