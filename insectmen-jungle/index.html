About the game

One kick at a time.

Jungle paths cross between the trees and ponds, and the insectmen come at you along them. Stop them with your kicks, push your score up and move from level to level.

The game tracks your kicks, kills and K/D ratio, and rewards you with skill points to spend on new moves.
