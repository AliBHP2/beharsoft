The story

A unique star in a vast universe.

Among the countless stars of one galaxy in this vast universe lies a unique star that humans settled hundreds of years ago. Why did people leave their beautiful homeland behind for this star?

Hadi is a boy born on this beautiful star. One day, strangers attacked his homeland and killed his parents — so he grew up strong, determined to free his star from them.

Join Hadi on his journey to free his star and his galaxy from evil, and discover this vast universe, the star, and the civilisation its human settlers built.
