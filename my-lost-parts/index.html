About the game

Help Goro find his parts.

Goro is tumbling through the sky, and his five parts are scattered across the map. You can't move him directly — instead you place a rock for him to land on and tilt it to steer where he goes next.

Every part you reach scores a base amount plus a bonus for how fast you were going when you hit it — the quicker the run, the higher the score.

How to play

One rock at a time.

⇆

Place a rock

Use Left / Right to place a new rock for the little monster to land on.

⇅

Tilt it

Use Up / Down to change the angle of the rock you placed.

➹

Give him a push

Press Space to give your little monster a push.

↺

Reset the rocks

Press Ctrl to clear every rock and drop a fresh one right under him.

◆

Five parts

Collect all five missing parts scattered across the map.

★

Speed scores

A base score for each part, plus a bonus based on your speed when you hit it.
